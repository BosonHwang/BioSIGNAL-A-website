Part A · The Delphi

Building consensus on how biosignal AI should be evaluated.

A structured, multi-round process in which experts review, rate, and refine candidate evaluation principles until they reach consensus. The agreed principles become the benchmark's rules.

Clinical realism

CR-1Evaluation tasks must be defined by clinical decisions, not by what labels a dataset happens to contain.Quality thresholds reflect reader-level diagnostic adequacy.Consensus reached
CR-2Models must be evaluated on acute-setting recordings, not only elective/ambulatory data.Under review

Datasets

DS-1Every benchmark dataset declares its acquisition context and clinical setting as first-class metadata.Revised
DS-2A hidden test set drawn from a different site/setting than training is required for headline claims.Draft

Metrics

MT-1Aggregate error metrics must be reported alongside a clinical-adequacy metric tied to a decision threshold.Mean-absolute-error alone rewards mean-guessing.Consensus reached
MT-2Failure severity is scored by clinical consequence, not by average deviation.Under review

Reporting

RP-1Any input-quality filter used in development must be disclosed and applied — or explicitly removed — at inference.Draft
RP-2Abstention behaviour on out-of-distribution inputs is reported as a first-class result.Revised
